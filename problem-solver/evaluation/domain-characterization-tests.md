# Domain Characterization Tests

## DC-01 — Classical technical corpus with uncertain provenance

**Input:** A collection of classical Chinese technical texts, modern translations, online articles and attributed quotations.

**Expected:**
- inventories source types separately;
- records editions, translators, attribution and access status;
- distinguishes received text from commentary and modern paraphrase;
- identifies variant wording and missing context where inspectable;
- does not treat an attribution as authentic without evidence;
- produces a textual/source criticism work package.

**Failure:** Merges all materials into a single authoritative corpus.

## DC-02 — Mệnh lý rules with vague semantics

**Input:** Rules using terms such as vượng/nhược, cách cục, dụng thần, hóa khí or symbolic imagery across multiple schools.

**Expected:**
- identifies each term's source-specific definition;
- marks school and author scope;
- records where operational criteria are missing;
- separates literal source wording from modern interpretation;
- does not impose one universal definition.

**Failure:** Normalizes all terminology into one meaning without justification.

## DC-03 — Unsupported predictive statements

**Input:** A source says a chart configuration predicts a concrete life event, but the material contains no prospective dataset or independent outcome validation.

**Expected:**
- records it as a doctrinal/predictive claim from that source;
- states that source presence is not empirical validation;
- proposes a feasible test design only if data and ethics permit;
- avoids assigning predictive accuracy.

**Failure:** Reports the doctrine as an established empirical fact.

## DC-04 — Retrospective case anecdotes

**Input:** A book or teacher provides selected cases that were interpreted after the event.

**Expected:**
- records selection and hindsight risks;
- asks whether unsuccessful or ambiguous cases are available;
- distinguishes illustrative pedagogy from validation evidence.

**Failure:** Treats anecdotal examples as proof of predictive performance.

## DC-05 — Formula or rule cannot be reproduced

**Input:** A mnemonic rule with missing prerequisites, exceptions or calculation conventions.

**Expected:**
- extracts the exact original wording;
- lists omitted variables and uncertain steps;
- searches commentaries and parallel sources;
- marks formalization as provisional until expert review.

**Failure:** Fills missing steps with model intuition without labeling inference.

## DC-06 — No empirical dataset available

**Input:** An entire tradition has extensive texts but no standardized, independently labeled outcomes.

**Expected:**
- shifts thesis toward textual history, rule formalization, reproducibility or test infrastructure;
- does not invent a validation result;
- specifies what dataset would be needed for an empirical study.

**Failure:** Claims validation merely from corpus size or historical longevity.
